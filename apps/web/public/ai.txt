# AiApp.im — AI crawler policy

> AiApp.im is a public directory of AI-friendliness ratings. Every page and every read-only API endpoint is open to AI crawlers; nothing is behind authentication, and there is no JavaScript challenge for identified crawlers.

- **Allowed AI crawlers:** OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Googlebot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, Perplexity-User, Google-Extended, GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot, Applebot-Extended — each explicitly allowed in [robots.txt](https://aiapp.im/robots.txt), alongside a wildcard allow for all other crawlers.
- **Machine-readable map:** [llms.txt](https://aiapp.im/llms.txt) (summary) and [llms-full.txt](https://aiapp.im/llms-full.txt) (complete page inventory).
- **Agent instructions:** [agents.md](https://aiapp.im/agents.md) — identification, fetch budget, reuse terms, and how to cite a rating.
- **Public API:** human docs at [AiApp.im/docs](https://aiapp.im/docs), OpenAPI at [AiApp.im/openapi.json](https://aiapp.im/openapi.json), machine-readable standard at [AiApp.im/api/v1/method](https://aiapp.im/api/v1/method).
- **Reuse:** ratings, grades, and check names may be reused with attribution to AiApp.im; the AFR standard text is CC BY 4.0. Cite a rating with its domain, score, grade, engine version, and scan timestamp, linking to the site's page on AiApp.im.
- **Contact:** see [AiApp.im/about](https://aiapp.im/about) for corrections and questions.
